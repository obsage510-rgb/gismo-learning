About Gismo

Technology that adapts to the student — not the other way around.

Millions of visually impaired, deaf or hard-of-hearing, and neurodiverse students face rigid digital classrooms every day. Gismo reimagines the learning workspace from the ground up with assistive tools woven into every lesson, quiz, and study session.

Hands-Free Voice Workspace

Navigate chapters, trigger AI explanations, and complete full interactive quizzes using natural voice commands and spoken feedback.

Adaptive Reading & Library

Switch seamlessly between high-contrast dark mode, OpenDyslexic typography, scaled text, and instant public-domain classics.

Educator & Student Sync

Instructors can publish custom quizzes, assign coursework, and track student mastery and accessibility accommodations in real time.

Our Vision

A world where every classroom is universally accessible by default.

We believe assistive technology shouldn't be an expensive add-on or an afterthought. Our vision is to equip schools, universities, and independent learners everywhere with a free, cloud-synced AI learning companion.

1. Zero-Barrier Access

Keep core AI tutoring, voice navigation, and classic textbooks open and free for students across every region.

2. Dignity Through Personalization

Empower each student to choose how they read, listen, and test — whether visual, auditory, or kinesthetic.

3. Open Knowledge Bridges

Connect public-domain literature and STEM curricula directly to real-time comprehension quizzes and educator analytics.

Transparency & Payment Guide

Frequently Asked Questions About Donating

Everything you need to know about our local currency & cryptocurrency payment rails, how the step-by-step demo works, and where every contribution goes.

Local Currency & Cards
How does the Local Currency (Paystack) payment section work?
▼

Our Local Currency & Card option is powered by Paystack and supports both Nigerian Naira (NGN ₦) and US Dollars (USD $).

  • Supported Channels: Visa, Mastercard, and Verve debit/credit cards, instant Nigerian bank transfers (NIP), USSD codes (e.g., *737#), and Mobile Money.
  • One-Time vs. Monthly: You can make a single gift or set up a recurring monthly sponsorship for sustained school support.
  • Instant Receipts: A verified reference code (`GISMO-...`) and receipt are generated immediately upon completion.
Cryptocurrency • Zcash
How does the Crypto (Zec.rocks / ZEC) payment section work?
▼

Our Cryptocurrency rail uses Zcash (ZEC) connected via Zec.rocks (`zec.rocks:443`) for fast, borderless, and privacy-preserving educational philanthropy.

  • Shielded & Unified Pools: Choose between Zcash Shielded (Unified), Sapling, or Transparent pools. Shielded transactions use zero-knowledge proofs so donor balances remain private while funding public good.
  • Wallet Deep-Linking: Click "Open in Zcash Wallet" to pre-fill the amount and encrypted student memo in Zashi, Zingo, or YWallet, or copy the receiving address directly.
  • On-Chain Verification: Paste your 64-character transaction ID (`txid`) to verify block confirmation and log your gift.
Fund Allocation & Impact
What and where do my donations go to?
▼

100% of every contribution goes directly toward student accessibility and inclusive education:

  • 40% — Neural Voice & AI Tutoring Credits: Pays for real-time ElevenLabs natural voice narration and 24/7 Gemini AI tutoring for blind, low-vision, and auditory-impaired students.
  • 30% — Community Study Hubs & School Onboarding: Funds assistive hardware, teacher training, and student workshops at The Block Hive (Nsukka), Uhere Study Center (Nsukka), and partner inclusive schools.
  • 20% — Accessible Library & Curriculum Digitization: Expands full-text public-domain literature, OpenDyslexic typography tools, and voice-navigable STEM quizzes.
  • 10% — Zero-Barrier Cloud Infrastructure: Keeps student accounts, voice-dictated notes, and cross-device syncing completely free with zero ads or paywalls.
Interactive Demo
How does the 4-step donation demo work? Will I be charged?
▼

The donation widget is currently running in Interactive Demo Mode so judges, educators, and visitors can experience the complete 4-step donor journey without spending real money:

  • Step 1 (Method & Amount): Pick Local Currency (NGN/USD) or Crypto (ZEC) and preview your real-time student impact.
  • Step 2 (Donor & Cause): Choose which accessibility fund receives your gift and enter your details (or click "Auto-Fill Demo Info").
  • Step 3 (Demo Checkout): Simulate a Paystack card/bank/USSD payment or click "Generate Demo TxID" to simulate a 64-character Zcash transaction.
  • Step 4 (Official Receipt): View your instant receipt—which is also saved to the Gismo database and visible in the Admin Database Explorer!
Direct Sponsorship
Can I earmark my donation for a specific school or disability program?
▼

Yes! In Step 2 (Donor & Cause), use the "Where Should Your Donation Go?" dropdown to direct 100% of your gift to Neural Voice Credits, Nsukka Community Study Hubs & School Onboarding, or Accessible Library Digitization. You can also name a specific school or student cohort in the Quick Note field.

Security & Privacy
Are my payment details and donor identity secure?
▼

Absolutely. Gismo never stores raw card numbers or banking credentials—all local currency checkouts are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant Paystack channels. For donors who prefer complete financial privacy, our Zcash Unified Shielded pool encrypts transaction metadata on-chain while still allowing you to leave an optional public or anonymous note for students.
