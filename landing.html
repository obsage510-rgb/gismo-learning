About Gismo

Technology that adapts to the student — not the other way around.

Millions of visually impaired, deaf or hard-of-hearing, and neurodiverse students face rigid digital classrooms every day. Gismo reimagines the learning workspace from the ground up with assistive tools woven into every lesson, quiz, and study session.

Hands-Free Voice Workspace

Navigate chapters, trigger AI explanations, and complete full interactive quizzes using natural voice commands and spoken feedback.

Adaptive Reading & Library

Switch seamlessly between high-contrast dark mode, OpenDyslexic typography, scaled text, and instant public-domain classics.

Educator & Student Sync

Instructors can publish custom quizzes, assign coursework, and track student mastery and accessibility accommodations in real time.

Our Vision

A world where every classroom is universally accessible by default.

We believe assistive technology shouldn't be an expensive add-on or an afterthought. Our vision is to equip schools, universities, and independent learners everywhere with a free, cloud-synced AI learning companion.

1. Zero-Barrier Access

Keep core AI tutoring, voice navigation, and classic textbooks open and free for students across every region.

2. Dignity Through Personalization

Empower each student to choose how they read, listen, and test — whether visual, auditory, or kinesthetic.

3. Open Knowledge Bridges

Connect public-domain literature and STEM curricula directly to real-time comprehension quizzes and educator analytics.
